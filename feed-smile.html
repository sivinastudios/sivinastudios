Meet Smile

Some lights exist only to be seen.
Others exist to help others see.

Smile is the oldest light in Yumemori.

Born from the very first act of kindness, Smile quietly wanders the forest, leaving warmth wherever it goes. It asks for nothing, speaks without words, and reminds every traveler that even the smallest act of compassion can change the path ahead.

Whether guiding someone who feels lost, celebrating moments of joy, or simply sharing a peaceful silence, Smile is never far away. The Garden of First Kindness is a reflection of its heart—a place where every flower, lantern, and gentle breeze remembers that kindness has the power to grow.
