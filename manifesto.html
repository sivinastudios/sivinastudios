Why Sivina Studios?

Imagine a world that asks you to slow down.

A world that values quiet moments as much as extraordinary ones.

A world that believes wonder still has a place.

That is why Sivina Studios exists.

We do not create experiences to escape the world.

We create them to help people notice it again.

If, after meeting Sivina Studios, you leave hoping we succeed—even if you never become a player, a supporter, or an investor—then we have already accomplished something meaningful.

Because every world deserves the chance to be remembered.

“If you wish for something with all your heart, one way or another, it'll come true one day—and it'll happen in an unexpected way.”

A Note Before You Begin

Thank you for opening these pages.

This manifesto is not a promise of success.

It is a promise of intention.

Within these pages you will not find exaggerated claims, impossible ambitions, or carefully chosen words meant only to impress.

1

You will find something simpler.

The beginning of a studio that believes careful work matters.

That stories can leave us kinder.

That wonder is worth protecting.

That games can become places we remember long after we have left them.

This is not the story of what Sivina Studios has already achieved.

It is the story of what we hope to spend a lifetime becoming.

Welcome.

We Are an Experience Studio

Sivina Studios exists to create experiences born from dreams.

Many people dream and forget.

I dream and remember.

Places.

Faces.

Sounds.

Emotions.

Sometimes even scents.

They remain with me long after I wake.

Those dreams become stories.

Those stories become worlds.

Those worlds become experiences that others can one day explore.

Our work does not begin with trends.

2

It does not begin with market research.

It begins with memory.

Because we believe memories have power.

Whether they come from childhood, imagination, or dreams, memories shape who we are.

Through every world we create, we hope people slow down, explore, wonder, and discover what those memories mean to them.

We are not interested in creating noise.

We are interested in creating moments that remain with people long after they have finished playing.

Games are our medium.

Experience is our craft.

Our Purpose

Every meaningful journey begins with a question.

Ours was simple.

What if a game could leave someone feeling more complete than when they started?

Not because they defeated the final boss.

Not because they collected every achievement.

Not because they reached the end first.

But because, for a little while, they experienced a world that reminded them to notice.

To wonder.

To slow down.

To remember.

Our purpose is not simply to tell stories.

3

It is to allow others to experience the emotions that existed inside the dream itself.

Not simply to witness the story.

To feel it.

The quiet after waking.

The peace.

The wonder.

The mystery.

The feeling of visiting somewhere that somehow felt more real than reality.

Our Identity

Every studio eventually becomes known for something.

Some become known for technology.

Some for spectacle.

Some for commercial success.

We hope Sivina Studios becomes known for something quieter.

A place where curiosity is welcomed.

Where kindness is never considered weakness.

Where wonder is treated as something worth protecting.

Where every world is created with care.

Where every visitor leaves carrying something meaningful with them.

We are not defined by the number of games we release.

Nor by the awards we receive.

Nor by the size of our team.

4

We are defined by the care we place into every world we create and every person we invite into it.

If one day someone simply says,

“I'm glad Sivina Studios exists.”

Then we will have become exactly the studio we hoped to be.

The Worlds We Build

Yumemori is more than a setting.

It represents the belief that memory, dreams, nature, and spirit are connected.

Whether those connections are literal or symbolic is left for each person to decide.

We do not seek to answer every mystery.

Some mysteries deserve to remain mysteries.

Because imagination is not an escape from reality.

It is another way of seeing it.

We believe stories can encourage kindness.

Silence can be meaningful.

Wonder still has a place.

Even the smallest moments can stay with us for a lifetime.

Those beliefs became Sivina Studios.

Who We Create For

Sivina Studios is for those who notice.

For those who ask questions.

For those who wonder if there is more waiting just beyond the path.

5

For those who find beauty in quiet places.

For those who still believe kindness matters.

For those who seek meaning instead of noise.

If that sounds like you...

Welcome home.

Our Promise

Every experience created by Sivina Studios should leave someone with one simple feeling.

Not excitement.

Not spectacle.

Not distraction.

But wonder.

The kind that is difficult to describe.

The kind that quietly stays with you.

Long after the experience has ended.

Long after the screen has gone dark.

Long after the memory becomes your own.

A Final Thought

Every studio begins somewhere.

This is where ours begins.

There is still much to build.

Much to learn.

Many worlds still waiting to be remembered.

Whether Sivina Studios becomes large or remains small...

Whether our worlds reach hundreds of people or millions...

We hope every experience carries the same quiet intention.

To leave the world a little kinder.

A little slower.

A little more curious.

And to remind those who visit that wonder has never truly disappeared.

Sometimes...

it is simply waiting to be remembered.
