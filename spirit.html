The Spirit

Not a guardian. Not a god. A presence.

The Spirit is attentive, patient, curious, gentle, and ancient. It does not command or perform. It notices, waits, invites, and reveals.

The guiding rule: nothing moves unless it has a reason to.

Its body is memory, smoke, light, electricity, mist, and wind. Its eyes remain still; emotion is shown only through their brightness.

The Spirit belongs everywhere—Yumemori and beyond. Space and time are not a destination for it, but the condition in which it simply exists.
